Formula 1

Formula 1 (F1) is the highest class of international open-wheel, single-seater auto racing sanctioned by the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA). Each season, teams and drivers compete in a series of races, known as Grands Prix, to win two championships: the Drivers' Championship and the Constructors' Championship
